How to play

  1. Each cube has one amber face. Turn a cube so that face points UP, then slide/push it onto a glowing plate.
  2. Light every plate and the portal opens.
  3. Push / Slide one spare cube into the portal. That finishes the room. You do not need to move every cube.
  4. Fewer moves and a faster time earn up to three stars. Stuck? Press Z to undo or Restart.

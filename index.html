PARALLAX is a single-player 3D puzzle about rotation. Push a cube onto the plate with the marked face up, and the portal opens.

How a room works

Select and move

Pick a cube and slide it across the floor. Walls and other cubes block the way.

Rotate

One face is marked. The plate only responds when that face points up.

Reach the portal

Once the plate is active, the portal opens. Your time and moves are saved to your profile.
